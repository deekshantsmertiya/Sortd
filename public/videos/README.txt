Drop the homepage video files in this folder with exactly these names:

  video-1.mp4  ->  Behind the Scenes with Dubai's Skydiving Aerial Team
  video-2.mp4  ->  Inside the 24/7 Kitchen Baking 10,000 Rotis an Hour
  video-3.mp4  ->  Driving a Restored 1978 Land Cruiser Across Al Qudra Dunes
  video-4.mp4  ->  Exploring Dubai Creek by Night on a Traditional Wooden Dhow
  video-5.mp4  ->  How Young Skateboarding Crew Transformed Business Bay Parks

The 6th sample card (Secret Rooftop Coffee Roastery) has NO video on purpose,
so you can see the "Nothing to show here" message. To give it a video, open it
in Admin > Videos and paste a link (e.g. /videos/your-file.mp4 or an Instagram reel).

Best format for the story player:
  - Vertical 9:16 (1080 x 1920, or 720 x 1280)
  - MP4 file, H.264 video (plays on iPhone, Android and every browser)
  - 10 to 30 seconds long
  - Under 10 MB each (smaller = starts faster on phones)
  - Sound is optional: videos start muted, as browsers require for autoplay

Any other video can also be added from Admin > Videos by pasting a direct
.mp4 link or an Instagram reel link into "Video URL". A file in this folder
is linked as /videos/your-file-name.mp4
